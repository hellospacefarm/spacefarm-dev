ALpal Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Nothing. ALpal does not collect any user data. We don't track your activity, store your information, or keep any personal details about you.

What We Save

Nothing. We don't save any data about you or your usage of ALpal. Everything you do in the app stays entirely on your device.

What We Share

Nothing. We don't share any information with third parties because we don't have any to share. Your data is your own, and it never leaves your device.


If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to reach out to us through our contact page.

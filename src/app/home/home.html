SpaceFarm Development LLC

SpaceFarm Development is an independent software studio dedicated to building simple, purposeful applications.

Our mission is driven by real-world needs. My first app, ALpal, was inspired by my wife's gluten intolerance. While traveling together, we often faced the challenge of communicating her dietary requirements in countries where English wasn't widely spoken. ALpal was created to bridge that gap—helping travelers clearly communicate their needs and explore the world with confidence.
